Somatic mutations can have divergent influences on clones and individuals
A major challenge in the field is understanding the effects of mutant clones on the organismal level. Unlike germline variants, it is difficult to predict or measure the phenotypic impact of low VAF mutant clones on individual patients. Complex influences of clones on organisms that are not purely detrimental or adaptive may represent the rule and not the exception. Discord between clone fitness and organismal fitness is important but difficult to identify. Protective mutations in the liver provide clear examples of how increased clonal fitness would translate into increased organismal fitness, especially when the genes are well understood or have known germline genotype-phenotype relationships. However, variants in less-understood genes can cause increased clonal fitness that might even lead to increased organ fitness, but at the cost of reduced organismal fitness.
