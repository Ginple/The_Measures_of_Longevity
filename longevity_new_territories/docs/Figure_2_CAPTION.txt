Figure 2 Liver-specific Tbx3 deletion protects against MASLD. ( A ) Tbx3 mRNA levels in WT mouse livers after NC or WD feeding for indicated durations. ( B ) Tbx3 mRNA levels in Tbx3 fl/fl livers 1 week after AAV-TBG-Cre injection. ( C ) Experimental schema for diet-induced MASLD. ( D ) Liver weight (left), body weight (middle), and liver/body weight ratio (right) of Tbx3- KO mice on WD for 3 months. ( E ) Representative H&E images from mice from D . ( F ) Plasma ALT levels from mice from D . ( G ) Liver/body weight ratio of Tbx3 -KO mice on WD for 6 months. ( H ) Liver triglycerides from mice from G . ( I ) Liver cholesterol from mice from G . ( J ) Representative H&E images from mice from G . ( K ) NAS scores from mice from G . Significance of relative Tbx3 mRNA in A was calculated using a 1-way ANOVA with Dunnett’s post hoc test. * P < 0.05; ** P < 0.01; **** P < 0.0001. Scale bars: 500 μm, left panels; 100 μm, right panels.
