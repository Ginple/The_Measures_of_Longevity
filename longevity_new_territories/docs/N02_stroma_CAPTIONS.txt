Fig. 5: Depletion of non-haematopoietic senescent cells reduces Dnmt3a- mutant haematopoiesis. Full size image a , Schematic of the experimental design to deplete senescent p16-3MR MSCs using GCV. b , Number of live and dead MSCs after culture with control or Dnmt3a R878H/+ CM, treated with or without GCV. P values determined by two-way ANOVA with Sidak’s multiple comparisons test ( n = 4 biological replicates per condition). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. c , Schematic of the experimental design to generate Dnmt3a R878H/+ haematopoiesis in p16-3MR recipient mice followed by GCV treatment. Transplant was performed >5 weeks after poly(I:C) treatment of donor mice. d , Representative histogram (left) and MFI (right) of SA-β-gal in MSCs (CD45 − Ter119 − CD31 − CD51 + PDGFRα + ) in p16-3MR mice with control or Dnmt3a R878H/+ haematopoiesis after treatment with vehicle or GCV. P values determined by one-way ANOVA with Tukey’s multiple comparisons test ( n = 5 control vehicle, n = 4 control GCV, n = 5 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle, n = 7 Dnmt3a R878H/+ GCV biological replicate recipient mice per condition). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. e , Frequency of senescent MSCs (SA-β-gal hi p16 hi Bcl-xL + Bcl-2 + ) in p16-3MR mice with control or Dnmt3a R878H/+ haematopoiesis after treatment with vehicle or GCV. P values determined by one-way ANOVA with Tukey’s multiple comparisons test ( n = 5 control vehicle, n = 4 control GCV, n = 5 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle, n = 7 Dnmt3a R878H/+ GCV biological replicate recipient mice per condition). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. f , g , Frequency of donor (CD45.1 + ) haematopoietic cells in the BM ( f ) and HSC ( g ) compartments of recipient mice. P values determined by Brown–Forsythe ANOVA test with unpaired t test with Welch’s correction ( n = 5 control vehicle, n = 4 control GCV, n = 5 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle, n = 7 Dnmt3a R878H/+ GCV biological replicate recipient mice per condition). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. h , Schematic of the experimental design to deplete senescent MSCs using ABT-263. i , Number of live and dead MSCs after culture with control or Dnmt3a R878H/+ CM, treated with or without ABT-263. P values determined by two-way ANOVA with Sidak’s multiple comparisons test ( n = 4 biological replicates per condition). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. j , Schematic of the experimental design to generate Dnmt3a R878H/+ haematopoiesis in wild-type recipient mice followed by ABT-263 treatment. Transplant was performed >5 weeks after poly(I:C) treatment of donor mice. k , Representative histogram (left) and MFI (right) of SA-β-gal in MSCs in mice with control or Dnmt3a R878H/+ haematopoiesis after treatment with vehicle or ABT-263. P values determined by Kruskal–Wallis with uncorrected Dunn’s test ( n = 10 control vehicle, n = 9 control ABT-263, n = 8 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle and n = 10 Dnmt3a R878H/+ ABT-263 biological replicate mice). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. l , Frequency of senescent MSCs (SA-β-gal hi Bcl-xL + Bcl-2 + ) in mice with control or Dnmt3a R878H/+ haematopoiesis after treatment with vehicle or ABT-263. P values determined by Kruskal–Wallis with uncorrected Dunn’s test ( n = 10 control vehicle, n = 9 control ABT-263, n = 8 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle and n = 10 Dnmt3a R878H/+ ABT-263 biological replicate mice). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. m , n , Frequency of donor (CD45.2 + ) haematopoietic cells in the BM ( m ) and HSC ( n ) compartments of recipient mice. P values determined by Kruskal–Wallis with uncorrected Dunn’s test ( n = 10 control vehicle, n = 9 control ABT-263, n = 8 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle and n = 10 Dnmt3a R878H/+ ABT-263 biological replicate mice). Non-significant P values (P > 0.05) not shown. o , Number of haematopoietic colonies at passage 2 (P2) from recipient mice treated with vehicle or ABT-263. P values determined by one-way ANOVA with Fisher’s LSD ( n = 9 control vehicle, n = 6 control ABT-263, n = 6 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle and n = 8 Dnmt3a R878H/+ ABT-263 biological replicate mice). Non-significant P values ( P > 0.05) not shown. p , Correlation between the frequency of senescent MSCs and donor-derived BM cells (left), and frequency of senescent MSCs and donor-derived HSCs (right) in mice transplanted with control haematopoiesis. Significance determined by two-sided Pearson correlation ( n = 10 control vehicle, n = 9 control ABT-263 biological replicate recipient mice). q , Correlation between the frequency of senescent MSCs and donor-derived BM cells (left), and frequency of senescent MSCs and donor-derived HSCs (right) in mice transplanted with Dnmt3a R878H/+ haematopoiesis. Significance determined by two-sided Pearson correlation ( n = 8 Dnmt3a R878H/+ vehicle, n = 10 Dnmt3a R878H/+ ABT-263 biological replicate recipient mice). Dots represent individual biological replicate mice. Bar graphs show mean ± s.e.m. In box plots, the box represents the interquartile range, the median is indicated by the line inside the box, and whiskers extend to the minimum and maximum values. Source data
