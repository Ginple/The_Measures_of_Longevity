## Working rules

1. **Canonical text.**
   - An English chapter becomes canonical only when its key claims are bound to sources, in the same way as the [ch5 pilot](../../../knowledge_base/pilots/ch05_measurement_contract/README.md).
   - Until then, the Chinese draft stays the bound version, and the two must be kept in step by hand.
2. **Direction of derivation.**
   - New chapters (for example 6, 7 and 9, which inherit no numbered source chapter) are written in English first.
   - The Simplified Chinese edition is derived from the English text once it is frozen.
   - The existing Chinese demonstration drafts are source material for the English chapters, not originals to be translated line by line.
3. **Shared claim IDs.**
   - Both language versions cite the same `CLAIM_LEDGER` IDs and KERNEL anchors.
   - A number may appear in either version only if it can be traced to a ledger entry or a bound source excerpt.
4. **Terminology.**
   - [GLOSSARY_EN.md](GLOSSARY_EN.md) fixes one English term per concept, with its Simplified Chinese counterpart, owner chapter and anchor. A test checks it against KERNEL and the ledger.
   - New chapters use its terms. A new term is added there before it is used in more than one chapter.
   - Glossaries in uploaded knowledge-base files (for example V7.0 EN Appendix B) are candidates only.
5. **Internal documents.** The ledger, assessments, audits, and governance records may stay in Chinese. Reader-facing manuscript and paper text is English-first.
6. **Length (DEC-017, author instruction, 2026-11-27).**
   - Content decides length. No total word target and no per-chapter word target apply.
   - A full English edition of about 200,000 words or more is acceptable.
   - The former planning target of about 4,500 words per chapter (option E-2) is withdrawn. A separate abridged trade edition (E-3) is not planned at present. See ledger X157 and [market positioning §3.1](../../market/MARKET_POSITIONING_2026-11.md).
   - Content-driven does not mean unconstrained. Every draft must pass three tests:
     - (a) **load-bearing**: each section answers a reader question or carries a claim, and each claim is traceable to a ledger ID or bound excerpt;
     - (b) **no repetition**: a point is developed once, in the chapter that owns it, and later chapters point back;
     - (c) **right layer**: derivations, assay details and long evidence tables go to appendices or companion papers, not the running text.
   - The Volume Ⅲ protection rule still binds. Chapters 23–33 must stay at or above 28% of the actual English word count. If they fall below, stop expanding Volume Ⅱ.
7. **Reader-facing titles** (post-positioning restructure audit, F-08).
   - Working titles in OUTLINE_v1 are stable map keys.
   - Each English chapter gets a reader-facing title when it is drafted, as chapter 5 did ("Letting Numbers Answer the Questions They Can Answer").
   - Internal terms must not reach a reader-facing title unless the chapter defines them, for example "measurement contract", "capacity accounting", "state turnover". "LIFE OS" in chapter 30 must be replaced.
   - Reader-facing titles are candidates until the author confirms them.
8. **Audio-ready running text** (F-11). Human-narrated audio is a priority in the US market.
   - The running text must make sense when heard, without tables, formulas or "see figure".
   - State a formula in words in the text, and put the formula itself in back matter B3 (Methods Appendix).
   - A table may appear in print only if the text already carries its point.
9. **Detailed outline first** (F-02).
   - A chapter is drafted only after its entry exists in a detailed outline that follows the [specification](DETAILED_OUTLINE_SPEC.md).
   - Volume Ⅰ: [DETAILED_OUTLINE_VOL1.md](DETAILED_OUTLINE_VOL1.md).
10. **Ownership** (F-03).
    - A concept, invariant or recurring case is developed only in its owner chapter (specification §3).
    - Other chapters use it briefly and point back.
    - Proposals to move ownership are recorded in the specification with a reason.
