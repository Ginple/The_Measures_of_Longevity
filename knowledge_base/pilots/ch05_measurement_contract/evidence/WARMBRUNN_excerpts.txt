In the 2-year Comprehensive Assessment of Long-term Effects of Reducing Intake of Energy 2 (CALERIE-2) trial (n = 220), participants were randomized to 25% CR or control diet. The intervention targeted weight loss over the first 6–12 months, followed by a 12-month maintenance phase.
To assess weight-regain consequences, participants were stratified by weight trajectory regardless of randomization, and group differences were balanced by propensity score weighting.
Between months 12 and 24, most participants either maintained weight (n = 112) or continued to lose weight (n = 58), whereas a smaller group regained >5% of baseline weight (n = 20).
For this secondary analysis, 190 participants (n = 120 in CR group; n = 70 in control group) with available oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) data were included.
