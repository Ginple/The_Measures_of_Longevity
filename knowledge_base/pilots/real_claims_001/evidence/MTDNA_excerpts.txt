E-MTDNA-MODEL
Our analyses support a model in which individual blood cells randomly accumulate low levels of neutral mtDNA variation (that is, cryptic mutations), probably owing to replication-related errors, that are not detectable in bulk. However, with age-related expansion of individual cellular clones (by means of CH), these low-level cryptic variants become detectable and give rise to the observed accumulation with age.

E-MTDNA-BULK-LIMIT
