# Jonkman TH et al. Genome Med 2026;18:142, PMC13632308. Confirms the KERNEL §3.2 ranges (44–53%, 4–21%, 1–6%) that the abstract gives only as maxima.
Cell composition explained 44% of the variance in calendar age (Fig. 5A, panel "Age") and likewise 44–53% of the variance for all DNAmAges except DunedinPACE, for which 26% was explained (Fig. 5A, panel "DNAmAge").
For example, cell composition explained just 4% of the variance in Zhang's AgeAccel, but 21% in Hannum's AgeAccel.
To validate these findings in an independent dataset, we downloaded an external dataset of sorted blood cell types (GSE167998), and generated in silico artificial cell mixtures (Fig. 6B).
Across all health outcomes, adjustment for cell composition significantly attenuated the association of AgeAccels for 4 out of 6 clocks but at 4%, 6%, 7% and 2% for Hannum, Horvath, PhenoAge, and DunedinPACE, respectively, the effect was relatively minor (Fig. 7C).
For mortality, the attenuation ranged from 1% for Horvath to 6% for PhenoAge (Table 2).
